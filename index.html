Things to Do

Explore the best attractions and experiences across the British Virgin Islands. From pristine beaches to cultural landmarks, discover what makes our islands unforgettable.

The Baths

Virgin Gorda

Natural pools of crystal-clear water surrounded by massive granite boulders. A geological wonder and one of the Caribbean's most iconic destinations.

Nature Beaches

Anegada Flamingo Pond

Anegada

Witness vibrant Caribbean flamingos in their natural habitat. This shallow brackish pond is home to hundreds of flamingos and diverse bird species.

Wildlife Birdwatching

Copper Mine Point

Virgin Gorda

Historic ruins of a copper mine dating back to the 19th century. Enjoy panoramic views of the Sir Francis Drake Channel and surrounding islands from this elevated vantage point.

History Hiking

Sage Mountain National Park

Tortola

The highest peak in the British Virgin Islands offering stunning 360-degree views. Lush vegetation and well-maintained hiking trails showcase the islands' natural beauty.

Hiking Nature

Jost Van Dyke Island

Jost Van Dyke

Relaxed island vibes with pristine beaches and famous beach bars. Great Harbour offers authentic Caribbean culture and some of the friendliest locals you'll meet.

Beaches Culture

Callwood Rum Distillery

Tortola

The oldest operating distillery in the Caribbean, producing fine rum since 1760. Tour the historic facility and taste authentic Caribbean rum crafted using traditional methods.

Culture History

Virgin Islands National Park

St. John, USVI (nearby)

Pristine beaches, coral reefs, and tropical forests. Exceptional snorkeling opportunities and well-maintained hiking trails through historical plantation ruins.

Snorkeling Nature

Coral World Ocean Park

St. Thomas, USVI (nearby)

Interactive marine aquarium featuring Caribbean sea life, an underwater observatory, and touch pools. Perfect for families and marine enthusiasts of all ages.

Wildlife Family

About wgo

wgo—short for "Wa Goin' On?"—is your guide to experiencing the British Virgin Islands. Inspired by the spirit of Limin' Times, a beloved local circular that kept residents informed about current happenings, wgo continues that tradition in the digital age.

We believe in connecting people with authentic experiences. Whether you're a visitor seeking adventure or a local exploring your own backyard, wgo helps you discover what's happening in the islands.
